Professional Summary

Dr. S. Kalaimani is an accomplished zoologist, academician, and researcher with more than eight years of teaching and research experience, particularly in undergraduate and postgraduate education. He began his academic career as Assistant Professor at GTN Arts College, Dindigul (2017), and has been serving as Assistant Professor at JKK Nataraja College of Arts & Science (Autonomous), Komarapalayam since 2018. His expertise spans zoology, Biotechnology, Aquaculture, Genomics and Fishery Science with significant contributions to interdisciplinary innovations.
